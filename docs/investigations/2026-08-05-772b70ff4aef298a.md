## Lessons / follow-up
**New degraded-mode technique caveat:** for `query alert`-type monitors that self-recover (chronic transient spikes), the scan-diff's `last_triggered_ts` (= `overall_state_modified`) can fire on BOTH the alert transition and the subsequent recovery transition across two consecutive cutoff windows, producing two distinct hashes for one real-world episode. Before treating a new past-cutoff hit as a genuine new occurrence for a monitor that already fired last cycle, check `overall_state` at scan time: if `OK` and the KB's evidence metric shows no second breach in the window, treat it as the recovery half of the already-logged episode (false-alarm, no KB write) rather than bumping occurrences again.
