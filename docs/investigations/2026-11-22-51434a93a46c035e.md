### Key findings
- **One** Android crash tripped both monitors. The P1 fires on ≥1 crash/h, and at Sunday volume one crash pushes the 4h crash-free rate below 99%.
- The crash is entirely inside the Android framework, in the splash-screen-view handoff during launch (`syncTransferSplashscreenViewTransaction`). No app frames are in the trace.
- It is a **recurring** signature: 4 times in 30 days, roughly weekly, always Android 13 and always on OPPO/Realme (ColorOS-family) devices, at app launch.
- 11-20 (86c1e78f) was a different signature (iOS WKNSUndoManager).

### Likely cause
A device/OEM-specific Android 13 framework bug in the SplashScreen view transfer on launch. It hits a small set of OPPO/Realme devices. It is real, but low-volume (~1 user-crash per week). The likely mitigation is on the app side, in how the splash screen is configured or dismissed (for example the SplashScreen exit / `windowSplashScreenAnimatedIcon` handling). I haven't verified this, because the mobile repo isn't readable from this host.
