### Likely cause
A bulk SMS send (271 `sms/notification`) fanned out into 814 Twilio status callbacks. `SmsService.LogSmsStatusAsync` does a sync Redis HMGET via `CacheMapperProvider.Get`, which starves the thread pool (Busy 28 vs Min 4). Callbacks time out in the SE.Redis backlog. A shared `Twilio.Security.RequestValidator` also throws concurrency exceptions. The result was 23 × 500 to Twilio, relayed through the gateway.
