### Key findings
- At 16:25Z a bulk SMS send (100 `POST /v1/sms/notification` in one minute) triggered ~300 Twilio status callbacks over three minutes. This is the **second such burst today**; the first was 15:04Z (eb53c2da).
- This burst was worse than 15:04Z. The callbacks starved the thread pool:
  - 11 sync Redis HMGETs timed out at 10 s.
  - 7 callbacks failed Twilio signature validation on the shared HMAC.
  - 18 × 500 went back to Twilio via the gateway, which is what tripped 70171778.
  - Those SMS delivery-status updates were probably lost.
- The gateway 5xx rate was 20 per 5 min against a 24h-ago baseline of 1–3. 100% of it was the mobilenotifications sms/status route. The gateway itself is healthy.
- The Redis server isn't implicated. The 10 s p95 is the client timeout, and the exception's own counters show a client-side backlog.
- I couldn't attribute an account or user count.

### Likely cause
Bulk SMS sends cause bursts of Twilio status callbacks. `SmsService.LogSmsStatusAsync` does a **synchronous** Redis read (`IDistributedCache.Get` → `ExecuteSyncImpl`), so the bursts starve the thread pool (min worker threads 4). A shared, thread-unsafe `Twilio.Security.RequestValidator` also throws under concurrency. Together they return 500s to Twilio.
