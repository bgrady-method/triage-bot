### Likely cause
Two competing hypotheses, neither confirmed:
1. **Monitor mistuned for legitimate bursty traffic.** Rainforest appears to deliver webhooks in scheduled/batch windows (heavy at ~04:00Z and ~08:00Z UTC) rather than continuously; a 1-hour no-data threshold during "business hours" will legitimately trip whenever there's a quiet hour, which the 7-day baseline shows happens routinely.
2. **Genuine upstream delivery stoppage.** Rainforest (the external payment processor) stopped sending webhooks, or something upstream of `runtime-core-api` (gateway, DNS, firewall) is silently dropping this specific inbound path. Cannot be ruled out — deploy check blocked, no error signal either way to distinguish "nothing arriving" from "arriving and being silently dropped before the trace boundary."

Given this is `team:transactions`/`severity:p2` (payments-adjacent) and this is the monitor's first-ever fire, it's escalated for a human to confirm directly with the Transactions team / Rainforest rather than the bot asserting either hypothesis with unverified confidence.
