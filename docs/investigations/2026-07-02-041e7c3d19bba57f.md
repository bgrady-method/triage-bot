### Key findings
- **Root cause candidate identified:** miurl-agent makes a periodic outbound call to `microservices.methodlocal.int/account/v3/cluster/internal/account` (an internal ms-account-api cluster-lookup endpoint) and that call **fails 100% of the time it's attempted** — every hit on this resource_name is also an error.
- The overall service error-rate percentage (which is what the monitor evaluates) only crossed the 5% Warn threshold because miurl-agent's total traffic is tiny (~32 hits/10min) — 2 failing calls is enough to read as 6.25%.
- **This is not a new incident.** The specific resource's hourly error counts show a similar chronic pattern going back at least 72 hours, with no clear onset — this looks like a pre-existing, low-grade break that just happened to statistically cross the monitor's threshold this cycle due to sample-size noise, plus this may be the **first time this specific DD monitor (302048396) has existed/fired** in our tracked history (no prior incident-log entries).
- No 5xx cascade or other-service co-firing observed — this looks contained to miurl-agent's own dependency call, not a broader ms-account-api outage (ms-account-api's own health-check/latency monitors are all OK this cycle).
- Ownership is ambiguous: ownership.md maps the closest name match ("miurl") to team **Transactions**, but the monitor's own Datadog tags say `team:vae`. Surfacing both rather than picking one.

### Likely cause
miurl-agent (best-guess mapping: `legacy-miurl-consumer`) has a background call to an internal ms-account-api endpoint (`/v3/cluster/internal/account`) that has been failing on every invocation for multiple days — plausibly a stale/incorrect internal account reference, a permissions/auth gap on that specific internal route, or the endpoint having moved/been deprecated without the caller being updated. Because miurl-agent's overall traffic is so low, this chronic 100%-failure-rate dependency call periodically nudges the service's aggregate error percentage over the monitor's 5% threshold, producing intermittent Warn fires with no clear pattern.
