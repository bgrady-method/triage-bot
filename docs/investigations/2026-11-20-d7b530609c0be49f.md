### Key findings
- The monitor crossed warn because one bulk SMS send happened at 15:05:05Z: 276 messages created in the same second on one messaging service. It's the same event that caused the status-callback burst, the Redis errors, and the Twilio RequestValidator HMAC concurrency errors in group `eb53c2dae3ea83e7` (logged last cycle, 15:05Z).
- 40% of the burst came back undelivered, mostly 30006 (landline or unreachable carrier). That suggests the recipient list includes many landlines or invalid numbers. Twilio still bills undelivered segments (price -0.0083 USD each in the sample).
- The 24h undelivered rate is 31%, against ~16% on 11-17..18. Part of that comes from outside the burst too (~355 of ~1200 non-burst messages).
- Volume is about 2.5% over the warn line (1531 vs 1500), nowhere near critical (2000). The rolling-24h window means this Warn will clear by itself around 15:05Z on 11-21.
- No deploy correlation is possible: there's no TFS visibility, and methodcrm fetches return 403.

### Likely cause
A customer or workflow bulk SMS send at 15:05Z pushed the rolling 24h outbound count just over the 1500 warn threshold. The send is legitimate traffic rather than a platform fault. Two things are worth a human look: the list hygiene (40% landline/unreachable), and the thread-unsafe Twilio validator that the same burst exposed (eb53c2dae3ea83e7).
