## Lessons / follow-up
- **Monitor 70171778 is count-based, not rate-based.** During and immediately after a high-traffic incident it can fire on absolute 5XX volume even when the error *rate* is healthy. Consider pairing it with a rate guard or a short post-incident mute to cut aftershock noise.
- **Chronic blocker: ES/Serilog log sink 401 (`dev_team`) for ~16+ days.** Every #alert-system investigation is running DD-only with no app-log visibility. This is the single biggest drag on triage quality and should be prioritized for an auth-credential fix.
- **KB clause hygiene:** tighten `ki-runtime-core-ansible-redis-deploy-burst` over-broad `contains:"runtime-core"` match (see above).
