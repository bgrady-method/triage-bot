## Lessons / follow-up
- KB matcher gap: the text-only `match_kb.py` cannot match this entry when Slack body is empty (which is the case for every DD Slack-bot alert). Monitor-correlation is the workaround used in this investigation. A future enhancement would be to extend `match_kb.py` (or the prompt step 3) to also try matching by `last_triggered_ts` of any known-monitor-id listed in KB `match.any_of` clauses (e.g. parse `monitors/77419271` and probe DD monitor state). Today's investigation succeeded because monitor 77419271 is hard-coded familiar; for less-familiar monitors this fails silently.
- KB entry's `fix_status: needs-ops-decision` is unchanged since 2026-05-21 and we're now at 25 occurrences. The escalation here is process, not technical: Ben needs to pick between options (a)/(b)/(c) in the playbook. Worth surfacing in the next stability-review as a decision-overdue item.
