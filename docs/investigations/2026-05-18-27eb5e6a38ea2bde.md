### Likely cause
**Same root cause as Group A** — a 30-min-cadence monitor (Datadog or Centreon) that crossed threshold, fired four times on consecutive evaluations, recovered, and crossed threshold again four times on the next four evaluations. The recovery + recurrence shape suggests the underlying signal is itself periodic / batch-driven:

1. **Periodic batch job that occasionally exceeds a threshold** (e.g. nightly sync or hourly cleanup that has a runaway iteration set, sometimes taxing the SQL/Mongo cluster enough to trip a monitor for ~1.5h then recover).
2. **Intermittent infrastructure issue with self-recovery cycles** (e.g. a SQL connection-pool exhaustion that auto-recovers but then re-exhausts when the next batch of long-running queries arrives).
3. **A monitor with overly aggressive thresholds** that flaps on normal background noise spikes — but the consistency of the 4-fire episode length argues against pure noise.

The exact `XX:35/XX:05` minute boundary is suggestive of a monitor configured with `evaluation_delay: 0`, evaluating every 30 min starting on the hour + 5/35 mark. That's a specific DD monitor config we could identify.
