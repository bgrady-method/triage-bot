### Likely cause
The `nonprod/apps` service is generating Redis command errors at a rate that periodically trips monitor 227940275's 10-error/5min threshold. The "nonprod" naming despite `env:prod` tagging suggests this is a service that either (a) was misnamed during onboarding but does serve real prod traffic, or (b) is a non-prod service incorrectly tagged with `env:prod`. Without source-of-truth for what `nonprod/apps` is, we can't classify the underlying errors as customer-impacting or noise.

The runtime-core-api 9-error tail is consistent with chronic background Redis cache misses/retries during normal operation; it is not the driver of this alert.
