### Key findings
- The bare KB match against ki-2026-07-12 (rest-api tables-endpoint) is refuted — rest-api itself had only 1 error in-window, nowhere near concentrated.
- The real signature is on **runtime-core-api**, `/api/v1/image` upload endpoint: 21× `NullReferenceException` in `SecurityContext.ValidateRequestAsync`, called from `ImageController.UploadAsync`.
- Local (stale, 16-day-old) copy of `SecurityContext.cs` line 124 shows `rawToken.ToLower().StartsWith("bearer ")` with no null-guard on `rawToken` — consistent with the observed exception if the upload request arrives with no/null Authorization header.
- No prior incident-log mention of "ImageController" — genuinely novel finding, not a recurrence of anything on file.

### Likely cause
Unauthenticated or malformed image-upload requests (missing Authorization header) hit `ImageController.UploadAsync` → `SecurityContext.ValidateRequestAsync(rawToken, ...)`, which dereferences `rawToken` without a null check, throwing an unhandled `NullReferenceException` and surfacing as a 5xx.
