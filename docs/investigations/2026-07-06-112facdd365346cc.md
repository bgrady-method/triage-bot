### Key findings
- **Recovery confirmed.** ms-authentication-api and gateway error metrics both show continuous elevation from 01:24Z through ~04:21–04:30Z, then a clean drop to zero — no data points at all 04:30–06:00Z, and the live "now" query (11:09Z) is also empty. The event lasted **~3 hours** (01:24–04:30Z), roughly triple the ~1-hour window the original mid-incident snapshot could see.
- **Blast radius was wider than the original snapshot showed.** The original investigation (taken while the incident was still live) named 8 services. This resolution-wave view adds: gateway (now both 5xx and P95, not just P95), method-import-api, nonprod/apps, ms-documents-api, tables-fields (both p95 and error-rate), ms-scheduler-api (both P95 and error-rate), ms-email-api, method-mobile-notifications-api (both P95 and error-rate), and — notably — **signup**, a service in a completely different team (Growth) and product surface (pre-account) from the rest of the SQL-dependent stack. That signup was hit too, on the exact same day-timeline as its own prior first-ever-fire (see below), makes a coincidental-second-cause explanation unlikely.
- **Signup: this is its second occurrence, now with a plausible shared cause.** The 2026-07-05 investigation (`590dd72178d6d3b8`) explicitly held off writing a KB entry pending a second same-shape occurrence, since the evidence at the time (2 latency spikes, 0 errors, 0 logs) didn't point at any particular cause. This time the same p90-latency monitor (154252126) fired again, joined by 4 sibling signup monitors (unexpected-errors, high-error-rate, Availability SLO, high-avg-latency) landing in the identical 04:13–04:37Z window as the rest of the cascade's resolution tail. Given the timing match, the working hypothesis is that signup's backend shares the same SQL-tier dependency as the rest of the affected stack — not an independent signup-specific bug. Absolute volume stayed tiny (5 errors / 185 hits over 4h), consistent with the "extremely low-traffic endpoint" characterization from the first occurrence.
- **Availability SLO monitor (154229727) is still in Alert state** as of this scan (not self-recovered like every other member). This is an `error_budget(...).over("7d")` burn-rate monitor — it does not clear on its own the moment errors stop; it stays tripped until enough clean traffic passes to refill the 7-day budget. Given signup's very low absolute traffic, a handful of errors is enough to burn a disproportionate share of a thin budget. Flagging as open, not asserting it's a live ongoing problem.
- **No deploy correlation** for any of the newly-identified services (methodcrm GH_TOKEN 403 gap is standing — same limitation as the original report).

Continuation of the same event from `dd3912b47506342f`: TCP-reachability loss to SQL clusters c2/c3/c4 starting ~01:24–01:40Z, cascading into every SQL-dependent service (now confirmed to include gateway, import-api, nonprod/apps, ms-documents-api, tables-fields, ms-scheduler-api, ms-email-api, method-mobile-notifications-api, and signup), self-resolving by ~04:21–04:30Z as SQL connectivity was restored (root cause on the SQL/network side still not independently confirmed by ops — same open item as the original report).
