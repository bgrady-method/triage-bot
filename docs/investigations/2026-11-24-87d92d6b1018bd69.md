### Likely cause
A bulk SMS send (312 `sms/notification`) fanned out into 922 Twilio status callbacks. Both the send path (`TwilioSmsProvider.ValidateAsync`) and the callback path (`SmsService.LogSmsStatusAsync` via `CacheMapperProvider.Get`) make synchronous Redis HMGETs through `IDistributedCache.Get`. Under the burst this starves the thread pool (Busy 18–21 vs Min=4), so commands time out in the SE.Redis backlog. A shared `Twilio.Security.RequestValidator` also throws HMACSHA1 concurrency exceptions. The result was 37 × 500 to Twilio on `/v1/sms/status`, relayed through the gateway.
