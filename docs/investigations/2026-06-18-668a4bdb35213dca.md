- Sample event: `EventType: payment_method.replaced, AccountId: 135488` — an **inactive** account whose Rainforest webhooks keep arriving.
- 5xx are intermittent (one every ~10–19 min) and ongoing (last at 21:04:40Z). Each 500 → Svix marks delivery failed → retries → recurring bursts, exactly the documented retry-storm.
- Impact is bounded: only inbound webhooks for inactive accounts fail; active-account payment processing is unaffected. But it is a **payment**, **critical-path (runtime-core-api)**, **P2** signal that has fired ~hourly all day undetected by anyone watching #alert-transactions.

### Likely cause
Inbound Rainforest payment-webhook endpoint throws an unhandled `AccountNotActiveException` for inactive accounts, returning 500. Svix retries the (permanently un-processable) delivery, producing recurring 5xx bursts that trip monitor 295882867.
