### Likely cause
`FieldController.SaveFieldLegacyExtended` → `ViewService.EditView` (ViewService.cs:~383) calls `TryGenerateView`, which retries `ViewProvider.GenerateView` once after a 200ms sleep on failure (ViewService.cs:502-525). `ViewProvider.GenerateView` (ViewProvider.cs:310-329) delegates to the legacy `SyncServicesHelper.fnCreateViewFromTableName`; when it returns a non-"Success" message, both the initial attempt and the retry fail, and `EditView` throws `System.Exception("Failed to generate view {FriendlyName} after retry.")`. Four field-save operations against the `Customer` and `Entity` views hit this in a 28-second window. The underlying reason `fnCreateViewFromTableName` failed is not captured in any log field reachable this session.
