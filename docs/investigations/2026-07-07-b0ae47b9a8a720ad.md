### Likely cause
A client (single account/session, email `westbrookmanager@staplesoil.com`) repeatedly called `POST /api/v1/security/expiresessiontoken` with an empty `accountName`, which `AccountContext.InitAsync` cannot resolve, throwing an unhandled `AccountNotFoundExceptions` that surfaces as HTTP 500. Root cause of *why* the client's accountName was empty (stale/malformed JWT, session-teardown race, deactivated account) is not determined from available telemetry.
