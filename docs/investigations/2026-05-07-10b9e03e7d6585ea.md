### Key findings
- The dominant error pattern is `status:0` (TCP-level connection failure, no HTTP response) on `GET /gateway/syncutil/syncwidget/{account}/GetSyncWidgetInfoAsync`. Accounts affected: antaressaddle (CA, US), officefurniturecentreglasgow (Scotland, UK), dentmagicswltd (England, UK), upstakinc (Sinaloa, MX), back2internationalltd (London, UK). Cross-geography scope rules out single-ISP failure.
- Errors appear across 5 distinct accounts with diverse ISPs — points to a backend service being unreachable, not client-side.
- `i.clarity.ms/collect` POST failures (status 0) from PortalMobile (iOS, 2 accounts) are third-party analytics calls to Microsoft Clarity. These contributed to the per-account XHR error count but are not a Method service issue.
- `/apps/api/dashboardview/GetApps` failure (status 0, smilejv2, Japan) suggests brief broader connectivity disruption, not purely a syncutil issue.
- The monitor fired at 08:17:12 UTC and returned to OK — self-resolved in ~9 minutes.
- No errors found in DD for runtime-core-api, ms-gateway-api, or any other backend service during the window.
- No recent deploys on ms-sync-util, legacy-syncservice-api, ms-gateway-api, or method-platform-ui.
- The runtime-core deploy at 00:46 UTC (7.5h before the alert) is outside the 4h deploy-correlation window and unlikely to be the cause.

### Likely cause
The `status:0` errors on `/gateway/syncutil/syncwidget/*/GetSyncWidgetInfoAsync` across multiple geographies, resolving in ~9 minutes with no backend service errors logged, is consistent with a transient IIS application pool recycle on the `legacy` pool (which hosts `legacy-syncservice-api`, the likely backend for the syncutil route). A pool recycle drops active TCP connections (producing `status:0`) and recovers automatically within minutes. The `i.clarity.ms/collect` failures are a separate, unrelated third-party analytics issue that inflated the XHR error count but did not affect Method service availability.
